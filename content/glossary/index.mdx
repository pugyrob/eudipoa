**Where an instrument defines a term, we quote the instrument. Where a term is a practice label with no legal definition, we say so.** Terms are alphabetical.

## Authentic source

"A repository or system, held under the responsibility of a public sector body or private entity, that contains and provides attributes about a natural or legal person or object and that is considered to be a primary source of that information or recognised as authentic in accordance with Union or national law" <Cite id="reg-2024-1183" pin="Art. 3 definitions as amended" />. For company representation facts, the business register is the authentic source in substance.

## BRIS (system of interconnection of registers)

The Business Registers Interconnection System connecting Member States' business registers under Directive (EU) 2017/1132, operational since June 2017 <Cite id="dir-2025-25" pin="recital 1" />. The electronic EU Company Certificate must also be obtainable through it <Cite id="dir-2025-25" pin="Art. 16b(4)" />.

## Digital EU power of attorney

The template-based, digital-only instrument created by the new Article 16c of Directive (EU) 2017/1132, by which a company listed in Annex II or IIB authorises a person to represent it in cross-border procedures within that Directive's scope. Drawn up, amended and revoked under national requirements; authenticated by eIDAS trust services; compatible for use with the EUDI wallet; accepted as evidence of entitlement "as specified in the document" <Cite id="dir-2025-25" pin="Art. 16c(1)–(2)" />. Full treatment: [/poa](/poa).

## Electronic attestation of attributes (EAA)

"An attestation in electronic form that allows attributes to be authenticated" <Cite id="reg-2024-1183" pin="Art. 3 definitions as amended" />. See [Attestation of attributes](/eidas/attestation-of-attributes).

## EU Company Certificate

The harmonised, register-issued certificate of a company's incorporation and key register facts, accepted in all Member States as sufficient evidence at the time of issuance <Cite id="dir-2025-25" pin="Art. 16b(1)" />. Full treatment: [/certificate](/certificate).

## EUID

The unique European identifier for companies used in the system of interconnection of registers; stated on the EU Company Certificate <Cite id="dir-2025-25" pin="Art. 16b(2)(d)" />.

## European Business Wallet

A Commission **proposal** (COM(2025)0838, procedure 2025/0358(COD)) for a regulation establishing business wallets. Not adopted law. Tracked at [/eidas/business-wallets](/eidas/business-wallets).

## European Digital Identity Wallet (EUDI wallet)

The wallet each Member State must provide under Article 5a of Regulation (EU) No 910/2014 as amended — enabling users to store, share and present person identification data and electronic attestations of attributes under their sole control, with selective disclosure <Cite id="reg-2024-1183" pin="Art. 5a(1) and (4)(a)" />.

## Legalisation / apostille

The formality chain by which one state certifies the authenticity of another state's public documents. The EU Company Certificate, the digital EU power of attorney and register-certified copies are exempt from "all forms of legalisation and any similar formality" <Cite id="dir-2025-25" pin="Art. 16d(1)–(2)" />. (The term "apostille" itself comes from the Hague Convention framework — a practice label here, not an EU-law definition.)

## Mandate (organisational)

A practice term, not a defined term of EU law: the authority relationship by which an organisation empowers a person or system to act for it. The nearest legal anchors are the attribute class "powers and mandates to represent natural or legal persons" <Cite id="reg-2024-1183" pin="Annex VI, point 9" /> and the power of attorney instrument itself. See [/mandates](/mandates).

## Qualified electronic attestation of attributes (QEAA)

An EAA "issued by a qualified trust service provider" and meeting Annex V requirements <Cite id="reg-2024-1183" pin="Art. 3 definitions as amended" />. Same legal effect as paper attestations; revocation is definitive from the moment it happens <Cite id="reg-2024-1183" pin="Arts. 45b(2), 45d(4)" />.

## Relying party

"A natural or legal person that relies upon electronic identification, European Digital Identity Wallets or other electronic identification means, or upon a trust service" <Cite id="reg-2024-1183" pin="Art. 3 definitions as amended" />.

## Transposition

The process by which Member States enact national measures giving effect to a directive. For Directive (EU) 2025/25: adopt and publish by 31 July 2027, apply from 31 July 2028 <Cite id="dir-2025-25" pin="Art. 4(1)–(2)" />. Status by country: [/poa/member-states](/poa/member-states).

## Trust services

Electronic services under Regulation (EU) No 910/2014 (eIDAS) — signatures, seals, timestamps and related services — used to authenticate both the EU Company Certificate and the digital EU power of attorney <Cite id="reg-910-2014" /> <Cite id="dir-2025-25" pin="Arts. 16b(6), 16c(1)" />.

## Wallet-relying party

"A relying party that intends to rely upon wallet units for the provision of public or private services by means of digital interaction" <Cite id="ir-2025-848" pin="Art. 2(1)" />. Registration regime: [/eidas/relying-party-registration](/eidas/relying-party-registration).
