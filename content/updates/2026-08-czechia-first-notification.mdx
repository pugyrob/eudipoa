**Czechia has become the first Member State to communicate transposition measures for Directive (EU) 2025/25: fourteen of them, per the EUR-Lex national-transposition record. Every one of the fourteen pre-dates the Directive. Czechia is not announcing a new transposing act; it is telling the Commission that existing Czech law already covers the ground.** The other twenty-six Member States show zero communicated measures <Cite id="nim-2025-25" />.

## What exactly was notified

The list spans thirty years of Czech legislation, all published in the Sbírka zákonů: the Notarial Code (Act No. 358/1992 Sb.), the Criminal Code (40/2009 Sb.), the Civil Code (89/2012 Sb.), the Business Corporations Act (90/2012 Sb.), the Public Registers Act (304/2013 Sb.), and amendments running up to Act No. 162/2024 Sb. on conversions of companies, published 19 June 2024 — still more than six months before the Directive appeared in the Official Journal. Notably present: Act No. 416/2022 Sb., Czechia's implementation of the previous digitalisation directive on digital tools and processes in company law. The full list is in [our archived snapshot](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/NIM/?uri=CELEX:32025L0025) and on the [Czechia page](/poa/member-states/czechia).

## What this does and does not mean

A notification is a Member State's own claim. EUR-Lex states that: Member States "bear sole responsibility" for this data, without prejudice to the Commission's verification of completeness and correctness <Cite id="nim-2025-25" />. So this does **not** mean Czechia has transposed Directive (EU) 2025/25. Provisions like the EU Company Certificate (new Article 16b) and the digital EU power of attorney (new Article 16c) did not exist in EU law before January 2025 <Cite id="dir-2025-25" pin="Art. 2" />; whether statutes from 1992–2024 genuinely satisfy them is exactly what a completeness check — the Commission's, and ours against the gazette texts — has to establish. Our [Czechia page](/poa/member-states/czechia) therefore records the status as "notified, not yet verified", and the [27-country table](/poa/member-states) keeps everyone else at Unknown.

It does mean transposition has formally started. Our weekly pass of the EUR-Lex record (the Publications Office updates it weekly) now has something to watch beyond zeros.
