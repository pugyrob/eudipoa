**As of 27 August 2026: the Directive is in force with its clocks running; the Commission's template implementing acts — due by 31 July 2026 — had not been located by us in the Official Journal as of our verification date; the Member State wallet deadline is under four months away; and the Business Wallet proposal sits in committee.** A status check across everything this site covers, so readers arriving now know exactly where the landscape is.

## The Directive's clocks

Directive (EU) 2025/25 entered into force on 30 January 2025 <Cite id="dir-2025-25" pin="Art. 5" />. Member States have until **31 July 2027** to adopt and publish transposing measures, applying them from **31 July 2028** <Cite id="dir-2025-25" pin="Art. 4(1)–(2)" />. Our [27-country table](/poa/member-states) records every Member State as Unknown: we have not yet verified any national transposing measure in an official gazette.

## The implementing acts question

The Directive required the Commission to adopt the implementing acts — including the templates for the EU Company Certificate and the digital EU power of attorney — **by 31 July 2026** <Cite id="dir-2025-25" pin="Art. 24(2), closing subpara., as inserted" />. That date has passed. On our verification date we searched EUR-Lex's legal-acts collection for the phrase "digital EU power of attorney": the only act returned is Directive (EU) 2025/25 itself, and a search for 2026 legal acts combining "power of attorney" with "template" returns nothing. On that basis: **as of 27 August 2026 the template implementing acts have not been published in the Official Journal.** We will not cite secondary reporting on this — either the acts appear on EUR-Lex or they have not been adopted. Watching for them is a standing item of our weekly source pass, and when they appear, the [wallet mapping](/poa/wallet), [granting](/poa/how-it-is-granted) and [revocation](/poa/revocation) pages will be rewritten from their text and this section updated.

If you have an Official Journal reference for these acts, we want it: [hello@eudipoa.com](mailto:hello@eudipoa.com).

## The wallet clock

Member State EUDI wallets are due within 24 months of the wallet implementing acts entering into force <Cite id="reg-2024-1183" pin="Art. 5a(1)" /> — which they did on 24 December 2024 <Cite id="ir-2024-2977" /> <Cite id="ir-2024-2981" />. That makes the deadline **24 December 2026**, under four months from this brief. The wallet-relying-party registration machinery applies from the same day <Cite id="ir-2025-848" pin="Art. 11" />.

## The Business Wallet proposal

Still a proposal. COM(2025)0838 was published on 19 November 2025; the parliamentary file (2025/0358(COD)) showed "Awaiting committee decision" on our verification date, with ITRE as the committee responsible <Cite id="oeil-2025-0358" />. Nothing adopted, nothing to comply with; our [tracker page](/eidas/business-wallets) carries the status log.

## What to watch next

In likely order of impact: the template implementing acts appearing in the OJ; the first national transposition measures (the Directive requires them to reference it on publication <Cite id="dir-2025-25" pin="Art. 4(4)" />, which makes them findable); Member State wallets shipping against the December deadline; and the ITRE committee report on the Business Wallet. Each gets a dated brief here when it happens, verified before it is written.
