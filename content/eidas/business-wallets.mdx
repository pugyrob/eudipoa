**The European Business Wallet is a Commission proposal for a regulation: COM(2025)0838, published on 19 November 2025, now in the ordinary legislative procedure as 2025/0358(COD). As of this page's last verification, the European Parliament's file shows the status "Awaiting committee decision". It is not law, its content can change, and nothing on this page should be relied on as if it were in force.** This page tracks the file because people already cite the proposal as if it were settled law.

## Who this page is for

Anyone planning around organisational digital identity who keeps hearing "the Business Wallet will handle that" — and wants to know what has actually been proposed, and where the file stands.

## What is on the table

The Commission's digital-strategy policy page describes a proposal for a regulation to establish European Business Wallets as a harmonised digital solution for businesses' interactions and operations across the EU <Cite id="business-wallets-proposal" />. The legislative file records: proposal COM(2025)0838, published 19 November 2025; referral to Parliament committees announced 19 January 2026; committee responsible ITRE (Industry, Research and Energy), with IMCO and JURI providing opinions <Cite id="oeil-2025-0358" />.

We do not summarise the proposal's articles here yet. The text keeps moving until at least the Parliament and Council positions exist, and summaries of draft articles go stale. When the file advances (committee report, trilogue outcome, adopted text) this page will be rewritten from the current text, and the changelog will say what changed.

## How it relates to what is already law

Three things are law today and do not depend on this proposal:

- The **digital EU power of attorney** and **EU Company Certificate** exist in an adopted directive with fixed deadlines <Cite id="dir-2025-25" pin="Arts. 16b–16c insertion and Art. 4" />.
- The **EUDI wallet** obligation on Member States — including for legal persons — and the attestation-of-attributes machinery are in force via Regulation (EU) 2024/1183.
- The **relying-party registration** regime applies from 24 December 2026 under IR (EU) 2025/848.

Whether the Business Wallet regulation, if adopted, becomes the organisational counterpart or container for any of the above is a question we will answer when there is adopted text to answer it from. What is certain now: none of the adopted instruments' obligations wait for it.
