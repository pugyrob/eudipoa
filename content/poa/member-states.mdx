**Member States must adopt and publish their transposing measures by 31 July 2027 and apply them from 31 July 2028 <Cite id="dir-2025-25" pin="Art. 4(1)–(2)" />. As of the last verification of this page, exactly one Member State — Czechia — has communicated transposition measures to the Commission, and none has been verified by us in a national official gazette.** Each country name links to a dedicated status page. Unknown means unchecked or not yet existing. It does not mean "no".

This table distinguishes two levels of knowledge: **notified** (the Member State has communicated measures on the EUR-Lex transposition record <Cite id="nim-2025-25" /> — official data, supplied by the Member State, not proof of complete transposition) and **verified** (we have read the measure in the national official gazette). No row reaches "verified" until the second thing has happened.

## How this table works

A row changes from Unknown only when we have opened the Member State's official gazette (or its official legislation portal) and read the measure. When that happens the row records the national measure's title, its official reference, its dates, and what it actually covers — Article 16c is only one piece of what has to be transposed. Secondary reporting, law-firm client alerts and conference slides never change a row. The Commission's transposition-monitoring data, when it appears on EUR-Lex under the Directive's national transposition tab, will be treated as a pointer to national measures, not as a substitute for reading them.

If you know of a published national measure we have missed, tell us — with the gazette reference — at [hello@eudipoa.com](mailto:hello@eudipoa.com).

## The deadlines the table is tracking

| Date | Obligation | Basis |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 31 July 2027 | Adopt and publish national measures; inform the Commission immediately | Art. 4(1) |
| 31 July 2028 | Apply the measures | Art. 4(2) |
| 1 August 2028 / 1 August 2029 | Later dates for Article 19(2), point (i), and Article 19b of Directive (EU) 2017/1132 (group-of-companies information) | Art. 4(3) |

National measures must reference the Directive on their official publication <Cite id="dir-2025-25" pin="Art. 4(4)" />, which is what makes gazette-level verification feasible: the reference requirement means a transposing act identifies itself.
