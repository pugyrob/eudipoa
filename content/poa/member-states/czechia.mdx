**Status: measures notified to the Commission; three of the fourteen now read in the official Czech collection, and none verified as complete transposition.** On the EUR-Lex national-transposition record, Czechia is the only Member State showing communicated measures for Directive (EU) 2025/25: fourteen of them <Cite id="nim-2025-25" />. Every one is a pre-existing statute. The newest, Act No. 162/2024 Sb. on conversions of companies, was published on 19 June 2024, before the Directive itself appeared in the Official Journal. Czechia has told the Commission that existing Czech law already covers ground the Directive occupies.

We have now read three of the fourteen in e-Sbírka, the official electronic collection of Czech law: the Public Registers Act 304/2013 Sb. <Cite id="cz-304-2013" />, the Notarial Code 358/1992 Sb. <Cite id="cz-358-1992" /> and the digital company law act 416/2022 Sb. <Cite id="cz-416-2022" />. The register machinery is largely in place, the five-working-day entry deadline is shorter than the Directive requires, and the notarial identity check already works remotely at the high assurance level. The EU Company Certificate, the digital EU power of attorney and the Article 16e contact point are not in those acts, and no statute published before 2025 could contain them. The article-by-article reading, with the Czech section numbers, is at [Czechia's 14 notified measures, read against the Directive](/poa/member-states/czechia-notified-measures).

A notification is not proof of complete transposition. The Commission's own check of completeness and correctness is expressly reserved on the EUR-Lex page, and eleven of the fourteen acts we have not read.

## The dates that bind Czechia regardless

These come from the Directive itself and do not depend on national action:

| Date | What | Basis |
| --- | --- | --- |
| by 31 July 2027 | Czechia must adopt and publish its transposing measures | <Cite id="dir-2025-25" pin="Art. 4(1)" /> |
| from 31 July 2028 | Czechia must apply them: EU Company Certificate issuance, digital EU power of attorney, no apostille on in-scope documents, the 15-working-day filing rule, partnership disclosure | Arts. 16b–16d, 15, 14a insertions; Art. 4(2) |
| 1 August 2028 / 2029 | Later dates for group-of-companies information via the register interconnection | <Cite id="dir-2025-25" pin="Art. 4(3)" /> |

National law must cite the Directive when the transposing measure is officially published <Cite id="dir-2025-25" pin="Art. 4(4)" />. That requirement is how this page will catch it.

## What companies in Czechia should do

Nothing is obliged yet. What is worth doing now — checking your register entries, mapping who may act for you, ignoring premature "compliance" offers — is the same in every Member State and is set out in [What to do, by when](/comply). The EU-wide instruments themselves are covered in [the power of attorney explainer](/poa) and [the certificate explainer](/certificate).
