**Status: one step taken, nothing notified, nothing in force.** On 3 July 2026 the Belgian federal Council of Ministers approved a preliminary draft law transposing one point of the Directive: Article 2, point (5), which is the preventive-control rule <Cite id="be-ministerraad-2026-07-03" />. That draft goes to the Council of State for an opinion and then through Parliament. It is not law. Belgium has communicated no measure on the EUR-Lex national transposition record <Cite id="nim-2025-25" />, and nothing has been published in the Belgisch Staatsblad / Moniteur belge that we have read.

## What the 3 July 2026 draft covers

The Council of Ministers' own announcement says the draft transposes Directive (EU) 2024/2810 on multiple-vote share structures, and Article 2, point (5) of Directive (EU) 2025/25, described there as the prior control of the instrument of constitution and amendments to the articles of association <Cite id="be-ministerraad-2026-07-03" />.

That description matches the Directive. Article 2, point (5) replaces Article 10 of Directive (EU) 2017/1132 with a new Article 10 on preventive control. Member States must provide for preventive administrative, judicial or notarial control, or a combination, of the instrument of constitution and statutes of companies listed in Annexes II and IIB, at formation and on any amendment <Cite id="dir-2025-25" pin="Art. 2(5), inserting Art. 10(1)" />. Annex IIB is the partnership annex. The Belgian entries in it are the vennootschap onder firma / société en nom collectif and the commanditaire vennootschap / société en commandite, and those are the two forms the announcement names.

The practical change in Belgium is narrow and specific. Companies requiring an authentic deed already go through a notary. A VOF/SNC or CommV/SComm can in principle be formed by private deed, with no prior check. The draft closes that gap.

## What the draft does not cover

Nothing else in the Directive. The announcement mentions no EU Company Certificate, no digital EU power of attorney, no once-only principle, and no register interconnection <Cite id="be-ministerraad-2026-07-03" />. Those are Articles 16b to 16g and they still have to be transposed separately, by 31 July 2027.

The announcement also does not say which authority will carry out the preventive control, what has to be filed, or what it will cost. The text of the draft has not been published. Until the bill is tabled in Parliament there is nothing further to read.

## Why this page still says nothing is in force

A preliminary draft approved by the Council of Ministers is a government decision to send a text to the Council of State. It creates no obligation on anyone. Three things would have to happen before Belgium's row on [the transposition table](/poa/member-states) changes: Parliament adopts the law, it is published in the Belgisch Staatsblad / Moniteur belge, and we read it there. Belgium notifying the measure to the Commission would move the row to "notified", which is a weaker statement than "verified" and is not the same thing as complete transposition.

## The dates that bind Belgium regardless

These come from the Directive itself and do not depend on national action:

| Date | What | Basis |
| --- | --- | --- |
| by 31 July 2027 | Belgium must adopt and publish its transposing measures | <Cite id="dir-2025-25" pin="Art. 4(1)" /> |
| from 31 July 2028 | Belgium must apply them: EU Company Certificate issuance, digital EU power of attorney, no apostille on in-scope documents, the 15-working-day filing rule, partnership disclosure | Arts. 16b–16d, 15, 14a insertions; Art. 4(2) |
| 1 August 2028 / 2029 | Later dates for group-of-companies information via the register interconnection | <Cite id="dir-2025-25" pin="Art. 4(3)" /> |

National law must cite the Directive when the transposing measure is officially published <Cite id="dir-2025-25" pin="Art. 4(4)" />. That requirement is how this page will catch it.

## What companies in Belgium should do

Nothing is obliged yet. If you run a VOF/SNC or a CommV/SComm, the one thing worth knowing is that forming or amending one by private deed is on its way out, and the timing depends on the parliamentary calendar rather than on the 2027 deadline. Beyond that, what is worth doing now — checking your register entries, mapping who may act for you, ignoring premature "compliance" offers — is the same in every Member State and is set out in [What to do, by when](/comply). The EU-wide instruments themselves are covered in [the power of attorney explainer](/poa) and [the certificate explainer](/certificate).
