**Status: Unknown — nothing notified, nothing verified.** On the EUR-Lex national-transposition record, Romania shows no communicated measures for Directive (EU) 2025/25 as of our last check <Cite id="nim-2025-25" />, and we have not found or verified any transposing measure in the national official gazette. Unknown means unchecked or not yet existing.

## The dates that bind Romania regardless

These come from the Directive itself and do not depend on national action:

| Date | What | Basis |
| --- | --- | --- |
| by 31 July 2027 | Romania must adopt and publish its transposing measures | <Cite id="dir-2025-25" pin="Art. 4(1)" /> |
| from 31 July 2028 | Romania must apply them: EU Company Certificate issuance, digital EU power of attorney, no apostille on in-scope documents, the 15-working-day filing rule, partnership disclosure | Arts. 16b–16d, 15, 14a insertions; Art. 4(2) |
| 1 August 2028 / 2029 | Later dates for group-of-companies information via the register interconnection | <Cite id="dir-2025-25" pin="Art. 4(3)" /> |

National law must cite the Directive when the transposing measure is officially published <Cite id="dir-2025-25" pin="Art. 4(4)" />. That requirement is how this page will catch it.

## What companies in Romania should do

Nothing is obliged yet. What is worth doing now — checking your register entries, mapping who may act for you, ignoring premature "compliance" offers — is the same in every Member State and is set out plainly in [What to do, by when](/comply). The EU-wide instruments themselves are covered in [the power of attorney explainer](/poa) and [the certificate explainer](/certificate).
