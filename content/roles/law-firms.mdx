**The immediate legal work is to separate the harmonised document from the national law governing authority, capacity and procedure.**

## Questions for each instruction

- Is the proposed act within Directive (EU) 2017/1132's company-law procedures?
- Who has legal capacity and authority to grant the power?
- Does national law require filing of the grant, amendment or revocation?
- What exact scope and type of representation should the document state?
- What evidence must the receiving authority retain?

Article 16c permits Member States to require filing in a register <Cite id="dir-2025-25" pin="Art. 16c(3)" />. The Directive harmonises cross-border acceptance but leaves material national-law questions in place.

## Dates to put in client advice

The main transposition deadline is 31 July 2027 and application begins on 31 July 2028 <Cite id="dir-2025-25" pin="Art. 4(1)–(2)" />. Those are not deadlines for a company to purchase software.

## Proposal watch

The EU Inc. proposal would reuse the digital EU power of attorney for specified procedures undertaken by the proposed company form <Cite id="eu-inc-proposal-2026" pin="Art. 31" />. It is not law and should remain in a separate advice column.
