**A wallet can present evidence of authority. The receiving system still needs to determine whether that authority covers the requested action and remains current.**

## Legal requirements and technical material

The Commission's eventual implementing act for the digital EU power-of-attorney template must cover compatibility with EUDI Wallets <Cite id="dir-2025-25" pin="Art. 24(2)(e)" />. That act was due by 31 July 2026 and had not been located in the Official Journal at the 4 September check.

Regulation (EU) 2024/1183 includes powers and mandates to represent natural or legal persons among the attributes that may be covered by electronic attestations <Cite id="reg-2024-1183" pin="Annex VI, point 9" />.

The EUDI Architecture and Reference Framework is a living technical baseline. Version 3.0.0 was published on 21 July 2026. It is technical documentation, not law <Cite id="eudi-arf" />.

## Implementation checklist

- Keep credential validity separate from authority evaluation.
- Carry issuer, subject, represented organisation, scope, validity and status.
- Support revocation and fresh status checks.
- Bind the relying party's request to declared purposes and requested data.
- Preserve an auditable result, including why an action was allowed or refused.
- Track the final Commission template act before claiming conformance.

## Do not claim

Do not call a private credential the official digital EU power of attorney before the legal template and national procedures apply. Do not call an ARF requirement a statutory obligation unless the legal instrument also supports it.
