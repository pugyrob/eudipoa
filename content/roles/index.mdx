**The work differs by role. Companies maintain accurate records and grant authority; advisers determine legal scope; notaries and registers perform national functions; relying parties decide whether to accept an action; identity providers carry and validate evidence.**

## The common control

Every role needs to keep three questions separate:

1. Who is the actor?
2. What company or person do they represent?
3. Does the current authority cover this exact action?

The power-of-attorney acceptance rule reaches only the entitlement specified in the document <Cite id="dir-2025-25" pin="Art. 16c(2)" />. The relying party remains responsible for authenticating and validating the presentation <Cite id="reg-2024-1183" pin="Art. 5b(9)" />.
