**Directive (EU) 2025/25 is law and must be transposed. EU Inc. is a proposed optional company form. The proposal borrows the Directive's certificate and power-of-attorney framework rather than replacing it.**

## The legal difference

Directive (EU) 2025/25 entered into force on 30 January 2025. Member States must transpose its main measures by 31 July 2027 and apply them from 31 July 2028 <Cite id="dir-2025-25" pin="Arts. 4–5" />.

COM(2026) 321 is at the proposal stage. Its text can change. It has no fixed calendar date for availability <Cite id="eu-inc-proposal-2026" />.

## The practical connection

The Directive inserts the EU Company Certificate and digital EU power of attorney into Directive (EU) 2017/1132 <Cite id="dir-2025-25" pin="Arts. 16b–16c" />. Proposed EU Inc. Articles 30 and 31 then apply those tools to the proposed company form <Cite id="eu-inc-proposal-2026" pin="Arts. 30–31" />.

The Directive remains the base instrument. EU Inc. would add a new user of that machinery.

## What not to combine

- The Directive's 31 July 2028 application date is not an EU Inc. launch date.
- The EU Inc. proposal does not make Business Wallets adopted law.
- An EU Inc. power of attorney would not create authority outside the procedures and limits stated in the governing instrument.
- Existing national company forms do not need to convert to EU Inc. to prepare for Directive 2025/25.

## Procurement rule

Separate requirements into three columns: adopted law, legislative proposal and technical draft. Do not let a product roadmap turn a proposed feature into a claimed legal obligation.
